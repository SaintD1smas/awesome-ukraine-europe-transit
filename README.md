## 🏢 Major Departure & Transit Hubs

### Kyiv (Ukraine)
- **Central Bus Station (Kyiv Tsentralnyi):** Nauky Ave, 1/2 (Demiivska Metro Station). Key long-haul departure hub to Warsaw, Berlin, Prague.
- **Bus Station "Kyiv" (Pivnichna):** Symona Petliury St, 32 (adjacent to Kyiv-Pasazhyrskyi Central Railway Station).
- **Dachna Bus Station / Zhytomyrska Metro:** Beresteiskyi (Peremohy) Ave, 142. Standard Western exit departure point.

### Lviv (Ukraine)
- **Lviv Central Bus Station (Stryiska):** Stryiska St, 109. Primary hub for direct routes to Kraków, Katowice, Wrocław, and Munich.
- **Bus Station #8 (Dvirtseva):** Dvirtseva Sq, 1 (directly in front of Lviv Main Railway Station).

### Warsaw (Poland)
- **Warszawa Zachodnia (Al. Jerozolimskie 144):** The largest international coach terminal in Poland, with direct connections to all major Ukrainian regional centers.
- **Warsaw Chopin Airport (WAW):** Direct coach stops at the Terminal 1 arrivals sector.

### Kraków (Poland)
- **Kraków MDA (ul. Bosacka 18):** Adjacent to Kraków Główny railway station.

## ✈️ Airport Transit Corridors

For international travelers flying into regional European airports:
- **Warsaw Chopin (WAW) ↔ Kyiv / Lviv:** Direct departures without transferring through Warsaw city center.
- **Warsaw Modlin (WMI) ↔ Lviv / Kyiv:** Regular shuttle coaches connecting Ryanair flights.
- **Kraków-Balice (KRK) ↔ Western Ukraine:** Rapid highway access via the Polish A4 motorway.
- **Chișinău International (RMO) ↔ Odesa / Kyiv:** The fastest road transit link for Southern Ukraine (3.5–5 hours to Odesa).

## 🧳 Luggage, Pet Regulations & Border Guidelines

1. **Passport Validity:** Biometric passports must be valid for at least 3 months beyond the planned date of departure from the Schengen area.
2. **Traveling with Pets:** EU entry requires microchipping, rabies vaccination, and a valid rabies antibody titration certificate issued at least 30 days post-vaccination.
3. **Cash Limits:** Up to €10,000 (or equivalent) per passenger without mandatory written customs declaration.
